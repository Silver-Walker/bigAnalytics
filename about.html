About Us

A Leading Provider

A Private Social Enterprise

BIG Ltd is a Consultancy Firm registered in Kenya under Company’s Act Cap 486 (Reg no 109883 in the year 2004). We have over 20 years’ experience and expertise in project planning and management; organization Development; Developing and financial systems and structures; Governance, policy analysis/reviews, impact measurement and associated technical support.

To offer consultancy services at the Macro and Micro Level, using a variety of customized tailor-made approaches for improved community wellbeing, good governance and accountability.

An entity that provides highly professional and comprehensive set of solutions to development actors from policy, strategy to delivery of impact for improved community wellbeing.
