Short-Term Consultancies

  • Hands on operational support to clients based on specified needs/ ToRs However, this is restricted to BIG’s strategic areas of focus and expertise.
  • Specific areas include Baseline & End line surveys, Policy Analysis Development & Reviews, Strategy Development (OCA’s, Strategic Plans, Resource Mobilization, Monitoring & Evaluation, External Relations Plans & Public Relations Plans)

Training, Coaching and Mentoring

  • Medium to long term accompaniment-based support/ back-stopping support on program management or organisational development including seasonal staff placements for onsite support.
  • Workshops and hands-on digital skills programs
  • Agri-business management training

Capacity Development

  • This entails undertaking capacity assessment and subsequent organisational development support to current and future BIG Clients and other service delivery actors.
  • These also include open or tailor-made training around Climate Resilient Agriculture, Livelihood improvement, WASH, Integrated Community Health and Nutrition, Inclusive education.

Business Development Services

  • This involves market driven BDS in areas climate resilient agriculture, food security and value chain development e-Commerce, Systems Digitization and Automation.
